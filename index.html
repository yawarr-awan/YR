Gentle, Joint-Friendly Movement
Because your joints are hurting right now, start light. For the first 1–2 weeks do just the Morning Joint Mobility and a short gentle walk. Add the strength and isometric work as you feel able. Move only within a comfortable, pain-free range, and stop if you feel sharp or increasing pain. If a joint is hot or swollen, rest it. Please check with your GP or a physiotherapist before starting, especially with your arthritis.

✨ Today's Brief instructions

What you want the brief to do. Today's and tomorrow's events and tasks are always appended below this, so it can't be broken by editing here. Leave blank — or hit Reset — for the default, which is a plain Today/Tomorrow list with a bullet per item.

🤖 Today's Brief model

Which Gemini model writes the brief. Automatic follows Google's current Flash model and is the right answer almost always — pick a specific one only if you want it, or if Google retires one and the brief starts failing.

A name that turns out to be wrong or retired costs one wasted request — the brief falls back to the built-in list rather than failing.

📋 Google's model list 🔑 API keys

You don't need a new key to change model. One Google AI Studio key covers every Gemini model, and YR already has one. If it ever needs replacing, it's a Worker secret (GEMINI_API_KEY) set in the Cloudflare dashboard — the app can't change it, and neither can Claude.

🔄 Cloud sync (on)

Keeps your record the same on every device, backed up to your own private Cloudflare backend behind your own sign-in. On by default — a device that isn't syncing is quietly keeping a different copy. You can stop it here if you ever need to.

📲 Install as an app

🧩 Arrange your cards

Turn this on and every card grows a small toolbar: drag it by the ⠿ handle, nudge it up or down, send it to another tab, or put it away. Today, Prayers, Progress and Settings can all be rearranged.

Put away

Kept on this device, like the folded/unfolded state — your arrangement never travels with your health data.

🎨 Appearance

Light or dark. It follows your device until you pick one here.

Size

How big everything is — text, cards and spacing all together. Handy on a big monitor.

🔔 Reminders

Get a browser notification for prayer times, task due times, and dhikr reminders — only while this app is open in a tab or installed. Off until you turn it on.

💾 Your data

Everything you log lives only on this device, inside this file's storage. It stays there — even if you close the page — until you clear it. Nothing is sent anywhere. Back it up now and then with Export.
