Your Plan — the full picture
A quick, honest word on the goal. Even at this aggressive ~1,300–1,400 kcal, losing the full 20 kg in 2 months isn't physically possible safely — that pace needs a bigger daily deficit than your body actually burns. What this plan can realistically deliver is about 1–1.3 kg a week ≈ 10–14 kg in these two months (often near the top of that early on from water weight), with the full 20 kg landing around months 3–4. That is genuinely excellent, sustainable progress. Because this deficit is aggressive and you have arthritis and take medication, please have your GP or a dietitian keep an eye on it.

1. Your daily targets

  • Calories: ~950–1,000 kcal from three meals + ~370 kcal from your daily milk, chai, whey and collagen ≈ ~1,300–1,400 kcal total. You burn ~2,300–2,400, so this is an aggressive ~1,000 kcal deficit — about as far as it's sensible to push.
  • Protein: ~85–110 g from food + ~45 g from your collagen & whey ≈ ~140 g a day — kept deliberately high to protect muscle at this low calorie level. Your creatine helps here too — take 5 g daily with water and stay well hydrated.
  • Daily supplements & drinks: collagen scoop (morning), 1 whey scoop, 5 g creatine, a glass of milk and a low-sugar milk tea — all on your Today checklist. Have the whey shake mid-afternoon to bridge the gap now that snacks are gone.
  • Water: 2.5–3 litres (8+ glasses) — even more important on a low-calorie plan.
  • Sleep: aim for 7–8 hours — poor sleep raises hunger hormones and stalls weight loss.
At this low calorie level: take a daily multivitamin, make protein and vegetables the priority in every meal, and if you feel dizzy, very fatigued or unwell, eat a bit more — don't push through. Please have your GP or a dietitian supervise a deficit this aggressive, especially with your arthritis and medication.

2. Eight golden rules

  • Protein at every meal — eggs, chicken, fish, yoghurt, paneer, lentils, chickpeas.
  • Fill half your plate with vegetables/salad — volume with almost no calories.
  • Cut the "white stuff" — less sugar, white bread, white rice, fried food and sugary drinks. Keep roti/rice to one small portion.
  • Cook with 1 tsp olive oil, not ladles of ghee/oil.
  • Anti-inflammatory heroes for your joints: oily fish (salmon, mackerel), walnuts/flaxseed, olive oil, turmeric, ginger, garlic, berries, leafy greens, green tea.
  • Drink water before meals — often stops you overeating.
  • Stop eating ~3 hours before bed — helps digestion, sleep and morning stiffness.
  • One "off" meal won't undo you — just return to plan at the next meal.

3. How this helps your arthritis

  • Less weight, less load: ~4 kg comes off each knee for every 1 kg you lose — the single biggest thing you can do for joint pain.
  • Anti-inflammatory foods (omega-3, turmeric, ginger, colourful veg) can calm the inflammation that drives arthritis pain.
  • Gentle movement keeps joints lubricated and builds the muscle that supports them — but always within a pain-free range.
